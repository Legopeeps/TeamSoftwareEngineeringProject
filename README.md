<h1>Dead Man's Hand</h1>
<p>Created for CMP2804 Team Software Engineering</p>

<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Card game where your goal is to reach zero cards in your hand. Some say it's similar to a Spanish number...</p>
<p>Five cards in your hand, match either the suit or number on top of the pile (hint: "8"'s are wild)</p>

<h2>Game Specification</h2>
<p><b>Players:</b> Four player (or one if you're CRAZY)</p>
<p><b>Genre:</b> Party game, card-based</p>
<p><b>Tools Used:</b> Unity (engine), Aseprite/Libresprite (art)</p>

<h2>Controls (Mouse ONLY) </h2>
<p>Mouse Button One (Left Click): clicking cards, scrolling through your hand, navigating the menu</p>
